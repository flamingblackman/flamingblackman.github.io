About

Support first. Systems next.

My background is hands-on help desk and end user support. That work taught me to slow down, find the actual problem, and leave a record someone else can follow.

I have worked in IT support roles at DecisionOne, Ellucian, Tiffany & Co., and Ovation Travel. The work included account access, troubleshooting, ticket queues, documentation, device support, and helping non-technical users work through a fix.

I am now building toward Automation Specialist and RevOps Analyst roles. The through line is simple: understand the process, clean up the handoffs, and make the record easier to trust.
